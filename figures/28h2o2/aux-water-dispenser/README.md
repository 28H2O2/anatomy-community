# AUX water dispenser: storage, heating and flow

Made by **H2O2** ([28H2O2](https://github.com/28H2O2)) with Anatomy in Codex. This community edition is entirely in English. The original Chinese website is maintained separately.

An interactive teaching cutaway of a top-loading, hot/ambient bottled-water dispenser. Select seven stages to explore the upper unheated reservoir, the independent hot tank, resistance heating, thermostat cycling, simultaneous lower refill and upper hot outlet, and the ambient bypass. An expandable whole-appliance figure shows where the assemblies sit. A separate evaporation model keeps solute amount constant while water volume decreases.

The owner confirmed the AUX YR-5-A model and hot/ambient functionality. The exterior references the owner's photograph; hidden geometry follows comparable mechanisms and is **not a factory teardown or measured specification**. Temperatures, capacities, timings and actual water-quality readings are deliberately not invented. Reheating count alone does not establish hazardous concentrations; the page separates thermostat reheating, evaporation concentration and storage hygiene.

This community version contains no iframe, outside-frame communication, fonts, images or external addresses in its generated pages. References below are documentation links only. It uses the published Anatomy kit revision recorded in figure.json; the four geometry modules used here match those of the original local build byte for byte.
